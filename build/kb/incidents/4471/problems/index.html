The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so a booking for 7 April goes out for 4 July. It reaches them through the app, by email, and through booking-v2’s form.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants on booking-v3 type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it day first. Same swap, different bug: only the form, only booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it.

one-day: the date moves by one day. “Tuesday 12 May” shows as “Monday 11 May”; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday. Nothing is swapped. That points to a time zone (a date stored as midnight in one zone, shown in another), not to the parser. The desk had listed these in desk-notes.json and footnoted them under parser, with no ticket; here they get one.

How the reports were split

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, and the ids that come twice are dropped.
  • The tenant decides, not intake’s tag. meta.intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser; the cause comes from the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts). The two disagree, and the tagged as column shows by how much.
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move counts under one-day, and also under the cause the desk files it under by tenant, the way the desk’s own table counts it.
  • Checked against the bodies: every body that names two dates has them day and month swapped (parser, hint) or one day apart (one-day). The few that name no dates stay with their tenant’s cause.

Lessons

  • A subject line says what the reporter saw, not what broke. Merging on it put three bugs in one ticket (decision 0001).
  • Count by the tenant, not by intake’s tag.
  • A footnote under a cause is a problem without a ticket.
